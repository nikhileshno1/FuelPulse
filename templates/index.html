How mileage is calculated
  • Full-tank Distance between two full-tank fills divided by all fuel bought after the first fill, up to and including the second. Partial fills in between are added to the fuel total. This is the accurate figure.
  • Approximate Distance between two valid readings divided by the litres bought at the later fill. It assumes fuel bought equals fuel burned, so partial fills make it unreliable. Use it only as a rough guide.
  • An invalid reading breaks the chain: no distance or mileage is calculated across it. Expense totals still include any record with positive litres and a non-negative cost.
