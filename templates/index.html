Calculations & Exports

The Science Behind Your Fuel Data

How FuelPulse calculates your figures and what downloadable files are provided.

🧮 Core Calculated Metrics

Full-Tank Mileage (km/L) — Exact & Verified Distance driven between two full-tank fills divided by all fuel purchased between them (including partial top-ups). The industry gold standard for true efficiency.
Running Cost per Kilometre (₹/km) Total fuel spend over a verified cycle divided by the distance traveled. Reveals the true operational cost of taking each vehicle on the road.
Approximate Mileage (km/L) — Single Fill Estimate Single trip distance divided by single fill litres. Marked as approximate because partial fills do not reflect total burned fuel.
Average Price per Litre (₹/L) Weighted average price paid across all fill-ups, showing price volatility trends over time.

📄 Included Downloadable Reports (CSV)

1. Vehicle Mileage Report (CSV) Vehicle code, fuel type, full-tank cycles, verified km/L, cost per km, and efficiency ranking.
2. Monthly Fuel Summary Report (CSV) Monthly fill count, total litres bought, total cost, average ₹/L, and monthly mileage averages.
3. Tank-to-Tank Cycles Report (CSV) Start and end dates, start and end odometers, intermediate partial top-ups, distance driven, and exact cycle economy.
4. Flagged Reading Audit Log (CSV) Quarantined entries with error descriptions (e.g. reverse odometer sequence) for fleet compliance and data cleaning.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is full-tank mileage more accurate than trip mileage?

Fuel tank shapes are irregular, so fuel gauges and single partial fills cannot accurately tell you how much fuel was burned. By filling to full twice, the litres needed to refill the tank exactly equal the fuel burned during that distance.

Can I still log partial top-ups?

Yes! If you add a partial fill (e.g. ₹500 of petrol), untick "Tank filled to full". FuelPulse stores the fuel and cost, and when you next fill to full, it combines all intermediate fuel to calculate exact mileage without missing a drop.

What if I accidentally enter a wrong odometer reading?

FuelPulse automatically checks the reading against previous logs. If an impossible number is entered, it flags the row in the "Needs Review" panel so you can fix or delete it without skewing your dashboard statistics.

Can I export my data for tax or accounting purposes?

Yes. Every report (Vehicle Breakdown, Monthly Expenses, Tank Cycles, and Flagged Entries) features an instant CSV download button ready for Excel, Google Sheets, or accounting software.

How mileage is calculated
  • Full-tank Distance between two full-tank fills divided by all fuel bought after the first fill up to the second. Partial fills in between are included in the fuel total. This is the exact verified figure.
  • Approximate Distance between two valid readings divided by litres bought at the later fill. Use it only as a rough estimate.
  • An invalid reading pauses calculation: no distance or mileage is calculated across it. Expense totals still include any record with positive litres.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions & Calculations

Why is full-tank mileage more accurate than trip mileage?

Fuel tank shapes are irregular, so fuel gauges and single partial fills cannot accurately tell you how much fuel was burned. By filling to full twice, the litres needed to refill the tank exactly equal the fuel burned during that distance.

Can I still log partial top-ups?

Yes! If you add a partial fill (e.g. ₹500 of petrol), untick "Tank filled to full". FuelPulse stores the fuel and cost, and when you next fill to full, it combines all intermediate fuel to calculate exact mileage without missing a drop.

How is Running Cost per km (₹/km) calculated?

Total fuel expenditure across verified cycles divided by the verified distance driven. This reveals the actual operating expense of running your vehicle.

What happens if an odometer reading is flagged?

If an entered odometer reading is lower than a previous reading, it appears in "Needs Review". It is excluded from verified mileage averages to prevent corrupted statistics, and you can edit or delete it anytime.

Can I export my data for tax or fleet bookkeeping?

Yes, in the Analytics tab, click "Download CSV" on any report (Vehicle Breakdown, Monthly Expenses, Tank Cycles, and Flagged Entries) for instant spreadsheet exports.
